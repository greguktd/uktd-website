Measuring guide

You need a soft tape measure (a dressmaker's tape is perfect) and, ideally, someone to help hold things steady. Measure the prosthetic itself, not your residual limb. Take the socket off if that is easier for you.

Socket length

Measure down the front of the socket from the top edge (the brim) to the bottom where it meets the pylon or foot. If the brim is higher at the back, use the front.

Circumferences

Starting at the brim, wrap the tape all the way around the socket at each measuring point we give you. Keep the tape snug but not tight, and level with the floor. Write down the full distance around.

Pylon

If you'd like the pylon (the tube below the socket) wrapped too, measure its length and the distance around it. Most pylons are the same all the way down.

Valves, buttons and cut-outs

Tell us where any valves, pin-lock release buttons or straps sit so we can leave them clear. "Valve on the inside, 6 cm from the bottom" is perfect.
